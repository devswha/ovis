### Added
- On an account with a signed list, adding a PC from a device that is in the list signs the PC
  into the list. The card **Add this PC to your account?** shows a six-digit number that the PC
  shows too: confirm it at the PC, then press **Add**. A PC whose QR code is scanned is added
  without a number, over the session the scan made. An account with no list adds a PC as before.
