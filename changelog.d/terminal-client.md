### Added
- `ovis tui`: the inbox in a terminal for herdr-core, with the agents that wait for you first, the
  selected agent's card answered by a digit, and its terminal one key away in hc-tui's sidebar,
  tabs and splits. It fits a phone's SSH app in one column and opens where you were after a
  dropped connection. Built from source for now ([docs/tui.md](docs/tui.md)).
