### Added
- In the Mac app, on an account with a signed list, **Add PC → Ovis relay → Invite a PC** makes an
  invite that adds one PC to the account with no sign-in on that PC, for a PC an agent sets up: a
  line that works once and ends within fifteen minutes, with the one line the agent runs. Open
  invites show the minutes left and **Revoke**; ended ones say **Expired**, **Used** or
  **Revoked**.
