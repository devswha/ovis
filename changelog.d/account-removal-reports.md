### Added
- Experimental, off by default (Settings → Experimental → Account: confirm removals on every PC):
  removing a device from your account shows, for every PC, whether that PC reports it removed, and
  the device that removed it brings the removal to each PC it connects to until every PC has it,
  even one whose mailbox never got it. "It was lost or stolen" when removing a device lists the
  devices it added, so you can remove them too, one by one.
