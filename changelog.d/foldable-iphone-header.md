### Changed
- On the foldable iPhone, the header controls move into the status bar's band. The open display
  puts them in the side column in landscape and in the top band in portrait, where the title row
  goes because the tab row says the same. The closed cover display puts the menu at the head of the
  tab row and search, Chat and Terminal and More in its side column. The connection shows as its dot
  alone there. No other device changes.
