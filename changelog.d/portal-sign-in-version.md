### Changed
- A Portal sign-in now needs Portal v2.7.3 or later on this PC, and **Start** says so on an older
  one. A start without a sign-in still needs v2.6.1, and so does the relay. Visitors on the short
  address sign in again after Portal restarts or registers again, such as after a network drop or
  a sleep; visitors on the address that holds the identity stay signed in.
