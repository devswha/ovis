### Added
- Send later (experimental, off by default): with Settings → Chat → Send later button on, a clock beside Send schedules the
  message for a time you pick, tomorrow at 9:00, or when the agent's plan limit resets. The PC
  keeps it and sends it then while a device has the pane open (otherwise it waits until one does),
  waiting for the agent's turn to end; it is not sent into a pane that is gone or now runs another
  session, and anything it cannot rule out (an earlier paste that may sit in the input) waits for
  your OK. Scheduled messages show above the
  chat's queued ones on every device, with Send now, Edit and Discard.
