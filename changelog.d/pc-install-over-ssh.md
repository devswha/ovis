### Added
- A PC reached over SSH whose Ovis cannot update itself (started by hand, or too old to take an update
  asked for from another PC) is put right from this PC: Ovis is installed there with its official
  installer at this app's release, or restarted through its own launcher, then updated and connected
  again. A PC with no Ovis yet can be given one the same way. SSH uses keys only, and a refusal says
  what to do.
