### Fixed
- A terminal swipe that turns back just before the finger lifts now glides the new way, not the
  way it went before the turn.
- The tap that stops a terminal swipe's glide no longer clicks the terminal: it does not raise the
  keyboard or send a mouse click to the program.
- A terminal swipe's glide no longer goes on scrolling when you come back to a page you put away
  mid-glide.
