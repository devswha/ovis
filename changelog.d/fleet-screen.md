### Added
- A Fleet screen (Settings → Devices and PCs → Open fleet, or the sidebar's Fleet machines caption)
  lists the fleet's machines with their state, why one cannot be reached, how it connects and when
  it was last seen. The owner confirms a batch after reading its machines' fingerprints, removes a
  machine and downloads new invites as one archive; a device that only watches sees the same lists
  without those controls, and a PC without `OVIS_TOKEN` says how to set it.
