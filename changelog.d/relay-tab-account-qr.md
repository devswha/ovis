### Added
- Settings → Devices and PCs → Add a connection → Ovis relay now has "Add with a QR code" whether
  or not the page is signed in: press Show the QR code and scan it with a device that is signed in
  to your Ovis account, and the PC joins that account with no number to type and no approval on
  the PC. The code is made only on that press and is ended when the dialog closes, another tab
  opens, the page was hidden for a minute, or after 30 minutes. The dialog then says which account
  the PC joined and which device added it, with "Not me: revoke every device"; a device that is
  not signed in is named as paired with this PC alone.
