### Added
- Fleet: `ovis fleet on` turns the fleet on and shows its recovery phrase once; `ovis fleet invite`
  makes one-time invites as files, or as one line on a terminal (`--line`); `ovis fleet confirm
  <batch>` shows a batch's new machines with their fingerprints and lets them in after you agree;
  `ovis fleet list` shows machines, open invites and batches waiting for you. A new machine waits as
  `pending-confirmation` until its batch is confirmed.
