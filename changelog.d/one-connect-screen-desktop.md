### Changed
- On a desktop browser, My PCs and Add a PC stand side by side: the list on the left scrolls on
  its own, and the Ovis relay, Tailscale and SSH tabs stay open on the right. Past eight PCs, My PCs
  has a search by name and, on a desktop, All, On and Off chips. In a browser, the code way is one
  small Pair with a code line that opens its field.
- In the phone app, opening a PC by its Tailscale, SSH or other address says it is opening, with
  Cancel, and a failed open now explains why (off, out of reach, not Ovis, a Tailscale address
  while Tailscale is off) instead of the app's raw error.
